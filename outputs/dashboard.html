Each dot is a country's six-year mean. This is the between-country picture; the models use within-country variation, so a pattern here need not survive fixed effects.

Read this before ranking anyone. A large divergence is not evidence of misreporting. Night lights miss agriculture, the informal economy and off-grid activity, and they pick up gas flaring and electrification. In the fixed-effects models neither statistical capacity (-0.00001, p = 0.995) nor accountability (-0.0048, p = 0.886) explains the gap. And the check model rejects the gap's own premise: β̂ = 0.056 against H₀ β = 1, t = -37.3, so GDP and lights do not move one-for-one. This dashboard is for choosing where to look, not for drawing conclusions.

World Bank WDI

On disk · 7 files

GDP (constant LCU and growth), agriculture share, electricity access, urban share, population, oil rents

GDP levels are in each country's own currency at its own base year, so they are never compared across countries — only differenced within one.

EOG VIIRS Nighttime Lights

On disk · 2056 MB

The night-lights side of every gap on this page

VNL v2.1 annual composites 2015–2021, average_masked band, 15 arc-second
7 annual rasters

Summed inside national borders with area-weighted zonal statistics. The 2017 composite changed detection sensitivity, which inflates growth for dim countries — hence the bright-cells series shown alongside.

https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnl/

World Bank SPI

On disk · 1.8 MB

Statistical capacity — the published index, its five pillars, and the rebuilt version used here

SPI_index.csv (published scores) and SPI_data.csv (indicator level, 2004–2024)
2016–2024

Pillar 5 is Dimension 5.2 alone, and two of its ten indicators describe the GDP series itself. Those two are removed in the rebuilt score, after reproducing the published index exactly.

https://github.com/worldbank/SPI

V-Dem

On disk · 406.4 MB

Political accountability, and its vertical, horizontal and diagonal components

Country-Year Full+Others v16, v2x_accountability
published 2026, covers 1789–2025

Varies about 4.6× more between countries than within them, so fixed effects identify it weakly.

https://v-dem.net/data/the-v-dem-dataset/

OxCGRT

COVID-19 policy stringency, 2020–2021

OxCGRT_nat_latest.csv, StringencyIndex_Average, daily → annual mean
daily 2020-01-01 to 2023-02-28
